📘 The Candidate Playbook

What You Need to Take Care of While Applying

Top engineering recruiters look for authenticity, measurable impact, and verifiable skill. Here are the 6 key principles to guarantee your application stands out.

📊
Rule 1

Quantify Your Impact & Technical Scope

Recruiters pass on vague bullet points like "built a web app". Instead, use the Action + Context + Metric framework: state the technologies used, architecture decisions made, and measurable outcomes achieved.

❌ "Worked on backend APIs and optimized database."
✔ "Engineered Node.js/PostgreSQL microservices handling 25,000 daily requests, reducing p99 latency from 450ms to 120ms through Redis caching."
🧠
Rule 2

The Truth About Keyword Stuffing

Old ATS tools parsed raw keyword counts. AIRIS does not use word counters. Our semantic model understands architectural concepts and skill context. Dumping a block of 50 disconnected buzzwords in white text or footnotes is flagged and does not boost your score.

💡 Pro-Tip: Mention technologies naturally within the context of your actual projects, libraries imported, and engineering challenges solved.
🎯
Rule 3

Check Mandatory vs. Optional Skills

In AIRIS, every job posting clearly distinguishes between Mandatory (deal-breaker) skills and Optional (bonus) competencies. Before submitting, review the target role's mandatory qualifications.

💡 Pro-Tip: If a role mandates Node.js + SQL, ensure your resume highlights practical experience in both. If you are missing an optional skill like Docker, you can still be shortlisted!
📜
Rule 4

Upload Verifiable Certifications

Have you completed an AWS Certified Developer exam, Google Cloud Engineer cert, Kubernetes CKA, or accredited technical courses? Upload the certificate PDF or credentials alongside your resume.

💡 Pro-Tip: AIRIS validates document authenticity and applies an automated score multiplier to verified credentials, giving your profile an immediate competitive edge.
📅
Rule 5

Strict Factual Dates & Authentic Repos

Always use accurate employment and graduation dates. Ensure your GitHub and portfolio links are public and working. AIRIS strictly preserves candidate career timelines and verifies repository commit histories.

💡 Pro-Tip: Honest timelines with a continuous trail of GitHub commits beat exaggerated resumes every single time.
🖥️
Rule 6

Assessment Day Preparation & Integrity

When invited to the proctored technical assessment, prepare your hardware in advance: test your webcam, microphone, and internet connection. Ensure you test in a quiet, single-monitor environment.

💡 Pro-Tip: Do not switch browser tabs, split screens, or copy-paste external AI snippets. The proctoring engine monitors browser focus to guarantee your integrity score remains at 100%!

⚖️ Ethical Architecture

What Our System Gives Recruiters for Ethical Hiring

We provide hiring teams with state-of-the-art facilities engineered to eliminate bias, protect honest candidates, and ensure merit-first hiring.

🙈

Blind Resume Evaluation Option

AIRIS provides recruiters with a 1-click blind review mode that redacts candidate photos, personal addresses, names, and age indicators during initial screening. Candidates are judged solely on engineering architecture, project achievements, and demonstrated code competency.

🛡️ Protects you from: Unconscious bias regarding demographic background or geography.
📐

Objective, Standardized Code Evaluation

Traditional technical interviews often suffer from interviewer mood variance or obscure brain-teaser questions. AIRIS runs standardized test suites against production problem sets, scoring code on algorithmic correctness, modularity, and runtime execution efficiency.

🛡️ Protects you from: Subjective interviewer bias and trick trivia questions.
🔍

Auditable Proctoring Without False Accusations

Rather than automatically failing a candidate for an accidental window blur or network stutter, our proctoring engine generates an auditable, timestamped log for human recruiter verification. Honest candidates are never unfairly disqualified by algorithms.

🛡️ Protects you from: Sudden automated disqualifications due to accidental browser clicks.
🌱

Zero-Ghosting & Constructive Career Roadmaps

We mandate that every candidate receives closure. Hired applicants receive calibrated digital offer letters; non-selected applicants receive a personalized AI Career Growth Roadmap detailing verified strengths and exact recommendations for missing skills.

🛡️ Protects you from: Weeks of silent rejection and demoralizing ATS ghosting.
