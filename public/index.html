Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our enterprise B2B hiring solutions and candidate evaluation

How does AIRIS help companies hire technical employees end-to-end? +

AIRIS delivers an autonomous 5-step B2B hiring pipeline: (1) Requisition calibration with mandatory skill gating and proctoring tiers, (2) AI semantic resume parsing and credential verification, (3) Autonomous anti-cheat technical interviews with browser telemetry, (4) Stack-ranked cohort calibration via the Composite Hiring Index (CHI) with salary benchmarks, and (5) Head-to-head candidate comparison with 1-click legal offer letter generation.

How does AIRIS evaluate candidates beyond simple keywords? +

Unlike traditional keyword filters that can be gamed or reject qualified talent over phrasing differences, AIRIS employs semantic similarity analysis combined with domain skill lexicons. It analyzes the context of a candidate's projects, responsibilities, and technical proficiencies against the actual job scope.

How does the anti-cheat proctoring system ensure candidate integrity? +

During technical assessments, AIRIS tracks real-time browser focus (tab switching, window minimization), clipboard operations (copy-pasting), and optional webcam/audio telemetry. These events feed into an auditable Integrity Score (0–100%) that weights directly into the candidate's Composite Hiring Index, ensuring fair and trustworthy evaluation.

How are mandatory and optional skills handled? +

Recruiters can categorize qualifications into mandatory (must-have) and optional (nice-to-have) skills. If any mandatory skills are missing, AIRIS explicitly flags the application with clear feedback, ensuring recruiters can instantly spot deal-breakers without disqualifying candidates invisibly.

What is the Composite Hiring Index (CHI) and how does it rank cohorts? +

The Composite Hiring Index (CHI) is a mathematically balanced metric: 35% Resume & Skill Fit, 45% Proctored Technical Assessment Performance, and 20% Integrity Score. This allows hiring committees to rank entire candidate cohorts on merit rather than subjective bias, calibrated directly against industry compensation bands (L3–L7).

How does the system validate uploaded certifications? +

Candidates can upload verifiable certificates alongside their resume. AIRIS inspects the documents for issuer authenticity and extracts the skills taught, automatically applying recruiter-defined certification weighting to boost candidate rankings for verified credentials.

What happens when a candidate is not selected for a role? +

AIRIS eliminates the traditional ATS silence. Non-selected candidates receive an AI-generated, constructive Career Growth Roadmap highlighting verified technical strengths and actionable skill growth recommendations, preserving positive employer brand equity.
