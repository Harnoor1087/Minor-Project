Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from hiring teams and candidates about the AIRIS platform.

How does AIRIS serve both recruiters and job seekers fairly? +

AIRIS balances the needs of both sides: recruiters receive fast, verified, anti-cheat talent evaluation with structured leveling; applicants receive objective evaluation based on demonstrated skill, verified certifications, and guaranteed feedback with zero black holes.

How does the semantic screening avoid keyword traps? +

Unlike old ATS filters that blindly search for specific buzzwords, AIRIS uses semantic embeddings that understand engineering context, project responsibilities, and architecture descriptions, ensuring qualified talent is never rejected over phrasing differences.

How does the proctored assessment protect candidate integrity? +

During technical evaluations, AIRIS monitors browser focus (tab switching, copy-pasting) and optional webcam telemetry. This generates an auditable Integrity Score (0–100%) that weights into the Composite Hiring Index (CHI), ensuring candidates who do their own work are properly rewarded.

What is the Composite Hiring Index (CHI)? +

The Composite Hiring Index (CHI) is a transparent weighted formula: 35% Resume & Skill Fit, 45% Proctored Technical Assessment Performance, and 20% Integrity Score. It enables unbiased, merit-first candidate ranking calibrated against industry compensation bands (L3–L7).
