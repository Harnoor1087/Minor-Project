The 5-Stage Recruitment Pipeline

A calibrated, data-backed talent pipeline that eliminates recruiter fatigue and delivers verified engineering finalists.

01
Stage 1: Requisition Calibration

Role Calibration & Qualification Gating

Recruiters configure technical job requisitions with precision boundaries. Rather than a flat list of keywords, AIRIS lets you distinguish mandatory (deal-breaker) skills from optional (nice-to-have) competencies.

🎯
Mandatory Skill Gating: Candidates lacking non-negotiable tech stack requirements (e.g. Node.js, SQL, Go) are automatically flagged with constructive gap notes.
🏆
Certificate Multipliers: Assign bonus score weight to verified industry credentials (AWS Certified Developer, CKA, GCP Professional Data Engineer).
🛡️
Proctoring Tier Selection: Choose assessment rigor per role level: Basic (browser telemetry), Standard (telemetry + tab tracking), or Strict (telemetry + audio + webcam proctoring).
02
Stage 2: Semantic Resume Intelligence

Semantic Multi-Vector Resume & Project Screening

Traditional ATS systems reject qualified engineers if they omit exact keywords, or let keyword-stuffers pass. AIRIS uses semantic embeddings to analyze contextual project depth, engineering architecture, and quantifiable business outcomes.

⚡
Zero Screening Backlog: Incoming resumes are parsed within 2.5 seconds, matching candidate project experience against the actual job scope.
🔍
Document & Certificate Authenticity: Inspects uploaded PDF credentials, validates issuer institutions, and scores verified competencies.
📈
Resume Fit Score (0–100%): Mathematically formulated based on mandatory alignment, preferred skills, and demonstrated project scope.
03
Stage 3: Assessment Integrity

Autonomous Anti-Cheat Proctored Technical Assessment

Shortlisted candidates undergo a structured coding and systems reasoning assessment. Our multi-modal proctoring engine monitors browser focus and environment telemetry to guarantee independent candidate work.

🖥️
Browser Telemetry & Tab-Switch Tracking: Detects and flags unauthorized window blurs, tab switching, and dual-monitor splits.
📋
Clipboard Paste Monitoring: Identifies external code pasting and external AI assistant injections with exact timestamps.
🔒
Auditable Integrity Score (0–100%): Generates a transparent, timestamped incident log for recruiter review, eliminating false positives.
04
Stage 4: Cohort Intelligence

Composite Hiring Index (CHI) & Leveling Calibration

Rather than subjective interviewer gut feel, candidates are stack-ranked on a live Talent Matrix using the calibrated Composite Hiring Index formula.

📐 The Composite Hiring Index (CHI) Formula:
Resume Fit (35%) + Technical Assessment (45%) + Integrity Score (20%)
📊
Market Engineering Leveling (L3 to L7): Automatically maps candidate scores to industry seniority tiers from Junior Engineer (L3) to Staff / Principal (L6–L7).
💵
Market Compensation Benchmarking: Calibrates competitive base salary and equity ranges based on candidate score percentile and role level.
05
Stage 5: Final Decision & Offers

Head-to-Head Candidate Comparison & 1-Click Offer Suite

Hiring committees can select finalists and compare them side-by-side across scores, code quality, and salary bands. With 1 click, dispatch customized, legally structured corporate offers.

⚖️
Head-to-Head Finalist Matrix: Side-by-side comparison across technical accuracy, problem-solving speed, and verified credentials.
📝
1-Click AI Legal Offer Letters: Dispatches professional enterprise offer letters containing position details, base salary, equity, and start date.
🌱
Constructive Rejection Roadmaps: For non-selected applicants, dispatches personalized career growth roadmaps, safeguarding employer brand reputation.
📋 Candidate Prerequisites

What AIRIS Requires From Students For Your Company

We hold applicants to high standards of authenticity and preparation before they ever reach your hiring pipeline. Here is what we mandate from every candidate.

💻

1. Hands-On Engineering Competency

We require students to demonstrate practical problem-solving in production languages (Node.js, TypeScript, Python, Java, SQL, Go) rather than theoretical trivia memorization. Candidates must pass timed coding evaluations with verifiable execution tests.

  • Algorithm & data structure problem solving
  • Clean code style, modularity, and error handling
  • Time & space complexity awareness
🔗

2. Authentic Repositories & Verified Dates

AIRIS strictly enforces background integrity. Candidates must link verified GitHub profiles with genuine commit histories and authentic career dates. Fabricated timelines or hallucinated skills are detected and flagged.

  • Valid public GitHub or portfolio links
  • Verifiable employment & academic dates
  • Zero tolerance for hallucinated credentials
🛡️

3. Single-Screen Assessment Compliance

Before beginning an assessment for your company, students must complete an automated device check verifying webcam, microphone, and single-monitor focus. Any attempt to dual-screen, copy-paste external solutions, or switch tabs triggers an audit flag.

  • Working webcam and microphone verification
  • Exclusive browser focus during technical test
  • Strict adherence to the ethical testing oath
📜

4. Cryptographically Validated Credentials

When candidates claim cloud certifications (AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, Kubernetes CKA) or university degrees, they must upload digital certificates. AIRIS verifies document issuers to ensure authentic credit.

  • Authentic credential document upload
  • Validation of issuing institutions
  • Merit boost applied strictly to verified certs
💡
